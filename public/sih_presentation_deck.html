TACTICAL INNOVATION

A.U.R.A. Architecture: Sacrificeable Acoustic Swarm

Instead of risking multi-lakh single-point scanners, A.U.R.A. deploys a swarm of low-cost, impact-hardened autonomous nodes dropped into rubble fissures by aerial drones.

1. Survivable Void Profiling

Active ultrasonic transceivers pulse internal cavity geometry (0.02m to 4.5m) to locate hollow physical spaces capable of sustaining human life.

40 kHz Resonant Acoustic Pulse

2. Seismic Tap Recognition

Piezoelectric geophone sensors filter ambient disaster noise to capture rhythmic survivor tap patterns and concrete micro-vibrations.

Bandpass Filtered Micro-Seismic

3. Zero-Grid Offline Beacon

Node instantiates an isolated local Wi-Fi Captive Portal & RF Mesh Beacon. Responders connect with any phone to view coordinates with 0% internet.

2.4 GHz Captive Offline Portal
